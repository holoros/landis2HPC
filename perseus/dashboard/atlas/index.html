Three-mode calibration degeneracy pathology (v1.0):
  1. Active-growth: very low θ freezes the model; guard requires ≥50% of plots with >5% growth.
  2. Empty-aggregator: per-plot pipeline failure yields LL=0 default; guard requires non-empty per_plot.csv.
  3. Sample-size: few successful pairs yield trivially small LL magnitudes; guard requires ≥300 paired observations.
Production calibrations: ME T2 per-species (LL=+34.2, n=612); GA T1 θ=0.30 (LL=+5.26, n=218; T2 deferred); WA T2 per-species iter1_cand11 (per-plot LL=-0.217 over n=805).
