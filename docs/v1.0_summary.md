## What changed from v1.0-rc1 to v1.0 final

The two T2 CMA-ES chains both completed during the day on 2026-05-17. Post-hoc analysis revealed a third T2 degeneracy mode that the v1.0-rc1 driver did not protect against — *sample-size degeneracy*. The relevant adjustments:

1. **WA T2 final vector is iter1_cand11**, not the CMA-ES-reported "best" (which was iter5_cand2 with negLL = −0.85 over only n = 2 plots — a sample-size artifact). The corrected best, selected by per-plot LL with a n_pairs ≥ 300 minimum, is iter1_cand11 at LL = −174.4 over n = 805 plots.

2. **GA T2 is deferred.** The 112-candidate GA chain produced a maximum n = 82 paired plots and a mean of 10.3; zero candidates had n ≥ 100. Production GA calibration in this release is Tier 1 uniform θ = 0.30 with cross-validated LL = +5.26. Root cause investigation is the highest-priority next step. Memo: `docs/GA_T2_failure_memo.md`.

3. **CMA-ES drivers patched** with `MIN_N_PAIRS = 300` guard so future T2 attempts (including the GA re-run) detect the sample-size degeneracy mode automatically.

4. **Methods paper Section 2.6 added** documenting the three-mode pathology taxonomy (active-growth, empty-aggregator, sample-size) as a paper-novel methodological contribution.

## Three-mode calibration degeneracy taxonomy (paper-novel)

| Mode | Mechanism | Symptom | Guard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active-growth | Very low θ → near-zero growth across horizon | LL minimum at trivial fit (predicted ≈ IC ≈ observed) | active-growth fraction ≥ 0.50 |
| Empty-aggregator | Per-plot pipeline failure → empty per_plot.csv → LL = 0 default | LL = 0 reported, no data underneath | per_plot.csv has ≥ 1 data row |
| Sample-size | Few plots succeed → trivially small LL magnitude | Total LL near 0 (or "best") from n = 2–50 plots | n_pairs ≥ 300 |

The recommended complementary safeguard is to optimize per-plot LL (LL/n_pairs) instead of total LL. The v1.0 driver retains total LL as the CMA-ES objective with the sample-size floor; future work will switch to per-plot LL directly.

## Cross-state directional asymmetry (anchor result)

Literature LANDIS-II Biomass Succession parameters are biased in opposite directions across the three regions:

- **Maine** (T2 final): species multipliers cluster at 0.84–2.26 (median ~1.30). Literature systematically under-estimated regional growth.
- **Washington** (T2 final): multipliers cluster at 0.31–0.91 (median ~0.55). Literature systematically over-estimated regional growth.
- **Georgia** (T1 production): θ = 0.30 uniform downscaling. Literature substantially over-estimated regional growth.

Calibration changes the 100-year per-cell biomass asymptote by:
- Maine: 7.5% (T2 vs T0)
- Georgia: 35% (T1 vs T0)
- Washington: 67% (T2 vs T0)

These are large enough to materially shift carbon-policy implications of any state-scale LANDIS-II projection.
